Press Conference by Prime Minister Takaichi regarding the Launch of a Suspected Ballistic Missile by North Korea

November 7, 2025
  • Tweet

[Provisional translation]

[Opening Statement by Prime Minister Takaichi]
A short while ago, North Korea launched a ballistic missile. It is believed to have fallen outside Japan’s Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ), and no damage has been confirmed at this time. In response to this situation, at around 12:38 p.m., I issued instructions to provide information to the public and to ensure public safety, among other matters. We will continue to gather and analyze information. That is all.
