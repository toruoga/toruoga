Congratulatory letter by Prime Minister Ishiba to Prime Minister Ramgoolam of the Republic of Mauritius

November 22, 2024
  • Tweet
[Provisional translation]

On November 22, Mr. ISHIBA Shigeru, Prime Minister of Japan, sent a congratulatory letter to Dr. the Honourable Navinchandra RAMGOOLAM, GCSK, FRCP on his appointment by the President as Prime Minister of the Republic of Mauritius based on the results of the general elections held on November 10.

In the letter, Prime Minister Ishiba congratulated Prime Minister Ramgoolam on his inauguration and expressed his respect for Mauritius’s steady development under a stable democracy, as well as his hope that efforts towards greater prosperity of Mauritius and the Indian Ocean region will be further promoted under the leadership of the new Prime Minister.

Prime Minister Ishiba noted that Japan and Mauritius have built good relations over the years, and also conveyed his willingness to work closely together to further deepen friendship and cooperation between the two countries, taking opportunities such as TICAD 9 and Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai next year.
