Statement by Prime Minister ISHIBA Shigeru

October 1, 2024
  • Tweet
Cabinet Decision
[Provisional translation]

Today, I have been appointed Prime Minister of Japan, and a new cabinet, based on the coalition of the Liberal Democratic Party and Komeito, has been inaugurated. I will dedicate myself fully to fulfilling my responsibilities.
 
I aim to shape and safeguard Japan’s future based on five key pillars: “upholding the rules,” “protecting Japan,” “protecting the people,” “protecting local communities,” and “protecting opportunities for youth and women.”
 
To achieve this, we will promote a politics that is truthful, courageous, and sincere—one that is humble, warm, and action-oriented, and that earns the understanding and empathy of the Japanese people.
 
I ask for your understanding and cooperation.
