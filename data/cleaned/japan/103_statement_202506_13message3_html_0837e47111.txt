Message of Condolences from Prime Minister Ishiba following the passing of H.E. Mr. Edgar Chagwa LUNGU, Former President of the Republic of Zambia
June 13, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
Following the passing of H.E. Mr. Edgar Chagwa LUNGU, Former President of the Republic of Zambia on June 5, Prime Minister ISHIBA Shigeru conveyed a message of condolences to H. E. Mr. Hakainde HICHILEMA, President of the Republic of Zambia.
In his message, he expressed his sincere condolences and paid tribute to the former President LUNGU for his significant contributions to the development of Zambia and to further strengthening of Japan-Zambia relations.
