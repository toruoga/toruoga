Congratulatory Message from Prime Minister Kishida to President-elect Sheinbaum of Mexico
June 3, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On June 3, following the election of Dr. Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo in the presidential election held on June 2 (local time) in the United Mexican States, Mr. KISHIDA Fumio, Prime Minister of Japan, sent a congratulatory letter to President-elect Sheinbaum.
In the letter, Prime Minister Kishida expressed his desire to work with Mexico as a Strategic Global Partner to further develop the traditional and strong bilateral relations.
