Message of Condolence from Prime Minister Ishiba following the passing of H.E. Prof. Dr. Horst KÖHLER, former President of the Federal Republic of Germany
February 4, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
Following the passing of H.E. Prof. Dr. Horst KÖHLER, former President of the Federal Republic of Germany, on February 1, Prime Minister Ishiba conveyed a message of condolence to H.E. Mr. Olaf SCHOLZ, Chancellor of the Federal Republic of Germany.
In the message, the Prime Minister expressed his sincere condolences and paid tribute to Prof. Dr. Köhler for his significant contributions in fostering the development of Japan-Germany relations.
