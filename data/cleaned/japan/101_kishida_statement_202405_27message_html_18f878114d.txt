Congratulatory Message from Prime Minister Kishida to President Azali of the Union of Comoros
May 27, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On the occasion of the investiture ceremony for the President of the Union of Comoros, which took place on 26 May, Prime Minister KISHIDA issued a congratulatory letter to H.E. Mr. AZALI Assoumani, President of the Union of Comoros.
In the letter, Prime Minister Kishida expressed his hope that under the leadership of President AZALI, peace and stability would be maintained in the Union of Comoros and that efforts for its further development as a democratic state would be advanced. He also conveyed his willingness to work closely with the President for further strengthening the bilateral relations in all aspects, including political, economic and international arenas.
