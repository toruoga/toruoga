Press Conference by the Prime Minister on the Handling of the Declaration of a State of Emergency
September 8, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(On the handling of the declaration of a state of emergency)
I have just held a meeting of relevant ministers to discuss the measures going forward. We plan to consult with the experts committee tomorrow over the areas and periods of the declaration of a state of emergency and priority measures to prevent the spread of disease. The Government will make decisions based on the results to be reached in the committee. In making the decisions, we will pay special attention to the status quo of the medical system while considering the opinions that were provided by experts today.
