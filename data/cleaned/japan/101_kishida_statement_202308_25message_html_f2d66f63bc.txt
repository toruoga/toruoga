Video Message by Prime Minister KISHIDA Fumio on the Occasion of Ukraine's Independence Day
August 25, 2023
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
From Japan, here I express my heartfelt congratulations on the Independence Day of Ukraine.
Japan will continue to strongly support Ukraine, both from the public and private sectors.
Japan is always standing by you, the Ukrainians, who are defending freedom, a basic value of us all.
