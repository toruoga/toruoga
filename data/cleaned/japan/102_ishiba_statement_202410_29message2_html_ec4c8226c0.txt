Message of condolence from Prime Minister Ishiba following the damage caused to the Philippines by Typhoon Kristine
October 29, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On October 29,2024, regarding the damage to the Republic of the Philippines caused by Typhoon Kristine, which occurred on October 22, 2024 (local time), Mr. ISHIBA Shigeru, Prime Minister of Japan sent the following message of condolence to H.E. Ferdinand R. Marcos, Jr., President of the Republic of the Philippines.
“I am deeply saddened and worried to learn the news that many precious lives were lost, many people are still missing and serious damages were brought in the vast areas of the Philippines due to the typhoon Kristine.
On behalf of the Japanese government and people, I would like to extend my heartfelt condolences to the victims and their families, and express my sympathy to those who were affected.
Japan is ready to provide the all possible assistance to the Philippines. Like the Philippines, we fully understand the hardship caused by severe damages of typhoons. Japan always stands with the Philippines in overcoming this time of difficulties.”
