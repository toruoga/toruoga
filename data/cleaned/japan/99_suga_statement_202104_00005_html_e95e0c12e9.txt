Press Conference by the Prime Minister regarding Mr. MATSUYAMA Hideki's Masters Tournament Victory
April 12, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
That was truly wonderful. His achievement really has given us courage and moved the hearts of people across Japan as the battle against the novel coronavirus becomes prolonged.
He is from a university in the Tohoku region. Thus I believe that he has also given an impact to the region towards its recovery from the Great East Japan Earthquake.
He is not just the first male Japanese golfer to win a major championship, but also the first Asian to win the Masters. It is a remarkable achievement. Mr. Matsuyama is still young and I look forward to his continued success.
