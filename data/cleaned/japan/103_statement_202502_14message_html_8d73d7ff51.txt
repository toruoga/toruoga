Message of Condolence from Prime Minister Ishiba following the passing of H.E. Dr. Sam NUJOMA, the First President of the Republic of Namibia
February 14, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
Following the passing of H.E. Dr. Sam NUJOMA, the First President of the Republic of Namibia, on February 8, Prime Minister Ishiba conveyed a message of condolence to H. E. Dr. Nangolo MBUMBA, President of the Republic of Namibia.
In his message, the Prime Minister expressed his sincere condolences and paid tribute to Dr. Sam NUJOMA for his significant contributions in fostering the development of Japan-Namibia relations.
