Congratulatory Message from Prime Minister ISHIBA Shigeru to President Dissanayake of Sri Lanka on the results of the general election in Sri Lanka
November 15, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On November 15th, following the outcome of the general election in Sri Lanka, Mr. ISHIBA Shigeru, Prime Minister of Japan, sent a congratulatory message to H.E. Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.
In his congratulatory message, Prime Minister Ishiba expressed his desire for further cooperation to realize a “Free and Open Indo-Pacific” under President Dissanayake's leadership.
