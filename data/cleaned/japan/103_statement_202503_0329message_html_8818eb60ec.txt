Message of condolences from Prime Minister Ishiba on the earthquake damage in the Kingdom of Thailand
March 29, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On March 28, Prime Minister Ishiba delivered the following message of condolences to H.E. Ms. Paetongtarn Shinawatra, Prime Minister of the Kingdom of Thailand, following the earthquake damage in the Kingdom of Thailand on the same day.
“I have been saddened by the news that the earthquake that struck in central Myanmar on March 28 has caused a massive damage. I extend our deepest sympathies to those who have been affected.
We stand with the Kingdom of Thailand, our good friend.”
