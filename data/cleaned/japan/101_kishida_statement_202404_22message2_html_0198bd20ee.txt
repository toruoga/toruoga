Message of condolence from Prime Minister KISHIDA following the damages caused by the heavy rain and flood in the Sultanate of Oman
April 22, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On April 22, regarding the damage caused by the heavy rain and flood that occurred in the Sultanate of Oman from 14 to 17 April, Mr. KISHIDA Fumio, Prime Minister of Japan, delivered the following message of condolence to H.M. Sultan Haitham Bin Tarik Sultan of Oman.
“On behalf of the Government and people of Japan, I would like to express my sincere condolences to Your Majesty, the Government and people of the Sultanate of Oman for the immense damage and number of casualties that were caused by the floods that have recently occurred in your country.
I sincerely hope for the quick recovery of those who are affected as well as for the prompt reconstruction of the affected areas.”
