Message of Condolences by Prime Minister Kishida to H.E. Mr. Recep Tayyip ERDOĞAN, President of the Republic of Turkey
October 17, 2022
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
I am deeply saddened to learn that many precious lives were lost due to the accident on October 14 at a coal mine in Bartin province of the Republic of Turkey. Japan stands with your country. On behalf of the Government and the people of Japan, I would like to express my heartfelt condolences to those who lost their lives and pray for the early recovery to those who were injured.
