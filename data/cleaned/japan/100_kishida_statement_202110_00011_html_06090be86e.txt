Press Conference by the Prime Minister regarding the Dissolution of the House of Representatives
October 14, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(Regarding his current state of mind ahead of the dissolution of the House of Representatives)
It is time for us to seek public judgement. I solemnly stand here today before you. 11 days have passed since I took office, and I have spent those 11 days explaining what this new Cabinet is going to do. I would like to continue to appeal to the people what we are going to do and what we are seeking to achieve during the upcoming election campaign period. As for the details, I will address at a press conference scheduled for later today.
