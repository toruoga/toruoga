Message of Condolences from Prime Minister Kishida regarding the Damage Caused by the Wildfires in the U.S. State of Hawaii
August 12, 2023
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
I would like to express my deepest sympathies to the people and the Government of the United States for the devastating damage caused by the wildfires in Hawaii. I hope the fires will be brought under control as soon as possible.
I send my prayers to the victims of the disaster and hope for the earliest possible recovery from this disaster.
