Message of Condolence from Prime Minister KISHIDA Fumio regarding the Train Crash in the Hellenic Republic
March 6, 2023
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
In the wake of the train crash on February 28, which resulted in casualties of many passengers including young people, Mr. KISHIDA Fumio, Prime Minister of Japan, sent message of condolences to H.E. Mr. Kyriakos Mitsotakis, Prime Minister of the Hellenic Republic, expressing sincere condolences to the families of the deceased and wish a speedy recovery to those injured.
