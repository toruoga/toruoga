Messages of Condolences issued by Prime Minister Ishiba following the passing of H.E. Dr. Punsalmaa Ochirbat, the First President of Mongolia
January 19, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
Following the passing of H.E. Dr. Punsalmaa Ochirbat, the First President of Mongolia on January 17, Prime Minister Ishiba conveyed messages of condolences to H.E. Mr. Ukhnaa Khurelsukh, President of Mongolia, and H.E. Mr. Luvsannamsrai Oyun-Erdene, Prime Minister of Mongolia. In the messages, the Prime Minister expressed sincere condolences and paid tribute to the first President Ochirbat for his leadership in Mongolia’s democratization and transition to a market economy and the establishment of the foundation for the development of Japan-Mongolia relations.
