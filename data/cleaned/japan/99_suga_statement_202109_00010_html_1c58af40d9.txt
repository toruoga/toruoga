Press Conference by the Prime Minister regarding His Visit to the United States and Other Matters
September 23, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(Regarding his visit to the United States)
One of the purposes of this visit to the Unites States is to participate in the four-nation meeting hosted by the United States for the first time involving Japan, Australia and India. The meeting will focus on important issues such as vaccines, new technologies and climate change, while exploring concrete ways to achieve a free and open Indo-Pacific region.
