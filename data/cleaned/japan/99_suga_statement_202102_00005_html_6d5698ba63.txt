Press Conference by the Prime Minister on the Resignation of Tokyo 2020 Organising Committee President
Speeches and Statements by the Prime Minister
Press Conference by the Prime Minister on the Resignation of Tokyo 2020 Organising Committee President
February 12, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(On the resignation of Tokyo 2020 Organising Committee President Mori and other matters)
I believe Mr. Mori expressed his intention to resign after giving the matter deep consideration on his own. I expect that the Tokyo 2020 Organising Committee will choose the new president in a transparent manner, based on relevant rules.
The Government of Japan has high expectations that the committee will as soon as possible rebuild itself as a renewed organization welcomed by the Japanese people and people around the world. We will make all-out efforts to ensure a safe and secure Games.
(On former Committee President Mori's successor)
I myself believe that the decision must be welcomed by the citizens and it must be made based on rules and transparency. I haven't spoken with Mr. Kawabuchi.
(On former Committee President Mori's successor)
As I just mentioned, the Organising Committee will decide on the succeeding president in a transparent manner, based on relevant rules. It is important that the committee make that decision in such a manner, in line with procedures the citizens can accept. Nothing has been decided beyond that.
(On the Prime Minister's alleged message to former Committee President Mori)
I firmly conveyed to him that it is critical to have an organization and decision-making process that is welcomed by the public. That is what I conveyed to him.
(On the consistency with the Prime Minister's response at the Diet)
That is totally different. As I am the Supreme Advisor to the Organising Committee, I believe I should comment on the decision-making to say that the decision will be made with transparency, based on the relevant rules.
(On the period until former Committee President Mori resigns)
I have already responded to that point.
(On the approval of the vaccine)
That is something we truly must welcome. I will work diligently so that the citizens can get the vaccine as early as possible.
