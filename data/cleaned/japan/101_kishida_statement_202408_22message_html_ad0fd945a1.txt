Congratulatory Message from Prime Minister KISHIDA to H.E. Mr. Kristen Michal, Prime Minister of the Republic of Estonia
August 22, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On August 21, Mr. KISHIDA Fumio, Prime Minister of Japan, sent a Congratulatory message to H.E. Mr. Kristen Michal, Prime Minister of the Repblic of Estonia, on his appointment.
In the letter, Prime Minister Kishida extended his congratulations to Prime Minister Michal, and welcomed the development of bilateral cooperation, including in the digital and cyber fields where Estonia has strengths. He also expressed his intention to further deepen cooperation with Estonia in maintaining and strengthening the free and open international order based on the rule of law, including the realization of a "Free and Open Indo-Pacific."
