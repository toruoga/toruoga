Press Conference by Prime Minister Kishida regarding the Launch of Ballistic Missiles by North Korea
November 3, 2022
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
Ballistic missiles were launched a short while ago. I regard the launch of ballistic missiles on consecutive days as an outrageous act that cannot be tolerated for any reason. I have already given instructions to confirm whether or not there has been any damage, to provide appropriate information to the public, and also to analyze information. I will confirm the details of the situation from now. I also would like to answer your questions after once again confirming the situation
