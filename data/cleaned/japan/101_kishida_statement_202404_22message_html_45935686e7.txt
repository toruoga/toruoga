Message of condolence from Prime Minister KISHIDA following the damages caused by the heavy rain and flood in the United Arab Emirates (UAE)
April 22, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On April 22, in the wake of the damage caused by the flood disaster that occurred in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), Mr. KISHIDA Fumio, Prime Minister of Japan, delivered the following message of condolence to H.H. Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, President of the United Arab Emirates, H.H. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President, Prime Minister and Ruler of Dubai and H.H. Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Vice President, Deputy Prime Minister and President of Presidential Court.
“On behalf of the Government and people of Japan, I would like to express my sincere condolences to Your Highness, the Government and people of the United Arab Emirates for the immense damage, caused by the floods that have recently occurred in your country, including the loss of lives.
