Press Conference by the Prime Minister on the Request to Declare a State of Emergency
May 20, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(On the request from the Okinawa Prefectural Government to declare a state of emergency)
We have just discussed how to handle the request by the Okinawa Prefectural Government to declare a state of emergency with relevant ministers, and we decided to consult with the expert committee tomorrow.
