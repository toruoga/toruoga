Press Conference by the Prime Minister on the Results of the Mayoral Election of the City of Yokohama
August 23, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
(On the results of the mayoral election of the City of Yokohama)
Although the results were very regrettable, the residents made the decision on various issues facing the city including the coronavirus issue and I would like to humbly accept the outcome.
