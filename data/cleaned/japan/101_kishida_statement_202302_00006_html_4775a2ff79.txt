Press Conference by Prime Minister Kishida regarding the Ballistic Missile Launch by North Korea
February 20, 2023
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
(On North Korea’s launch of intercontinental ballistic missiles and the Government’s response)
As the Ministry of Defense announced a short while ago, North Korea launched short-range ballistic missiles and I was also briefed on this matter. The latest missile launches by North Korea follow that of an ICBM (Intercontinental Ballistic Missile)-class ballistic missile the day before yesterday, and we have requested that an emergency meeting of the UN Security Council be convened. We remain committed to gathering intelligence, as well as to ensuring vigilance and surveillance, while also working in even deeper collaboration between Japan and the U.S., as well as among Japan, the U.S. and the ROK.
