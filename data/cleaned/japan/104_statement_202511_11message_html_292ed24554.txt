Message of condolences from Prime Minister TAKAICHI regarding the explosion that occurred in Delhi, the Republic of India
November 11, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On November 11, Ms.TAKAICHI Sanae, Prime Minister of Japan, delivered the following message of condolences to H.E. Mr. Narendra Modi, Prime Minister of the Republic of India, regarding the explosion that occurred in Delhi, the Republic of India yesterday.
“I am in deep sorrow to learn that many precious lives were lost in the explosion that occurred in Delhi, India.
On behalf of the Government and people of Japan, I would like to express my heartfelt condolences to the vic-tims and their bereaved families. I also offer my sincere prayers for the swift recovery of the injured.
Prime Minister TAKAICHI Sanae ”
