Congratulatory Letter from Prime Minister Ishiba to President-elect Trump of the United States of America
November 6, 2024
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On November 6th, Mr. ISHIBA Shigeru, Prime Minister of Japan, sent a congratulatory letter to The Honorable Donald J. Trump, President-elect of the United States of America. In this letter, Prime Minister Ishiba extended his congratulations on President-elect Trump’s victory in the presidential election. He also expressed that he would like to work closely together to further strengthen the Japan-U.S. Alliance, which is a top priority of his administration, and to realize a Free and Open Indo-Pacific.
