Press Conference by the Prime Minister on North Korea’s Possible Launch of a Ballistic Missile
September 28, 2021
Tweet
[Provisional Translation]
Early this morning, North Korea fired a projectile presumed to be a ballistic missile. In response to the incident, I have given instructions to dedicate maximum effort to gather and analyze relevant intelligence, provide the public with speedy and accurate information, ensure the safety of aircraft, vessels and other assets and take all possible measures to prepare for contingencies.
With heightened vigilance and surveillance, the Government is analyzing the details of the launch. Thank you.
