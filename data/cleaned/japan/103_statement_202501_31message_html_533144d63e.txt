Message of Condolences issued by Prime Minister Ishiba regarding the Aircraft Accident in Virginia, U.S.
January 31, 2025
Tweet
[Provisional translation]
On January 31, regarding the aircraft accident in Virginia, U.S., Prime Minister ISHIBA Shigeru sent the following messages of condolences to the Honorable Donald J. Trump, President of the United States of America.
“I am greatly saddened by the recent news of the tragic aircraft accident in Virginia.
I would like to express my deepest condolences to the victims of this heartbreaking accident and extend my sympathies to all those affected and the bereaved families of the victims.
Japan stands with the United States and its people at this difficult time.”
